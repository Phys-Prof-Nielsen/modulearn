Day 2: High-Frequency Verbs & Basic Conversation Patterns#

✅ 1. Goal for Today#

  • Master 10 essential high-frequency verbs (polite form)

  • Practice basic request and desire patterns (〜てください, 〜たいです)

  • Use verbs in simple question & answer mini-dialogues

✅ 2. Core Verbs (Polite Forms)#

✅ 3. Essential Patterns#

1) 〜たいです (I want to ___)#

  • たべたいです (I want to eat)

  • のみたいです (I want to drink)

2) 〜てください (Please ___)#

  • まってください (Please wait)

  • みてください (Please look)

✅ 4. Mini-Dialogues (Practice Speaking)#

Dialogue 1 – Going Somewhere
A: どこにいきますか？ (Where are you going?)
B: レストランにいきます。 (I’m going to a restaurant.)

Dialogue 2 – Eating Something
A: なにをたべますか？ (What will you eat?)
B: さかなをたべます。 (I’ll eat fish.)

Dialogue 3 – Request
A: まってください。 (Please wait.)
B: はい、わかりました。 (Yes, I understand.)

✅ 5. Exercises#

Exercise 1 – Translate into Japanese#

  1. I want to eat sushi.

  2. Please look.

  3. I drink coffee.

  4. There is a cat.

  5. I’m going to a park.

Exercise 2 – Verb Identification#

Which of these verbs describes an action vs existence?

  1. あります

  2. のみます

  3. います

  4. たべます

Exercise 3 – Make Your Own Sentences#

Write 3 sentences using:

  • 〜たいです

  • 〜てください

  • いきます or します

Exercise 4 – Speaking Practice#

  1. Repeat all 10 verbs aloud 5× each.

  2. Shadow Dialogue 1 & 2 3×.

  3. Record yourself asking:

    • どこにいきますか？

    • なにをたべますか？

✅ 6. Listening & Shadowing Practice#

(Use the MP3 you generated for Day 2)

  1. Listen through once without speaking.

  2. Shadow immediately after each verb/sentence.

  3. Play 3 times for each dialogue.

✅ 7. Review & Self-Check#

At the end of today, you should be able to:

  • Recognize and use 10 high-frequency verbs in polite form

  • Ask and answer basic questions about actions

  • Politely request actions and express what you want to do

from gtts import gTTS
from IPython.display import display, Audio
import io


# ✅ Day 2 – High-Frequency Verbs & Mini Dialogues
day2_sentences = [
    # --- Verbs ---
    "します。",       # do
    "いきます。",     # go
    "きます。",       # come
    "みます。",       # see, watch
    "たべます。",     # eat
    "のみます。",     # drink
    "いいます。",     # say
    "あります。",     # exist (objects)
    "います。",       # exist (people)
    "かいます。",     # buy
    
    # --- Example Patterns ---
    "たべたいです。", # I want to eat
    "のみたいです。", # I want to drink
    "まってください。", # Please wait
    "みてください。",   # Please look

    # --- Mini Dialogues ---
    "どこにいきますか？",    # Where are you going?
    "レストランにいきます。", # I’m going to a restaurant
    "なにをたべますか？",    # What will you eat?
    "さかなをたべます。"     # I will eat fish
    "まってください。",
    "はい、わかりました。"
]

# ✅ Generate and play each sentence
for i, sentence in enumerate(day2_sentences):
    print(f"Sentence {i+1}: {sentence}")
    tts = gTTS(text=sentence, lang='ja')
    mp3_fp = io.BytesIO()
    tts.write_to_fp(mp3_fp)
    mp3_fp.seek(0)
    display(Audio(mp3_fp.read(), autoplay=False))
