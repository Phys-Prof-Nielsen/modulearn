Day 4: Daily Life Vocabulary: Food, Shopping, and Useful Nouns#

✅ 1. Goal for Today#

  • Learn everyday nouns (especially food, drinks, and items)

  • Practice shopping and transaction phrases

  • Build confidence in asking for things, reading menus, and handling money

✅ 3. Useful Shopping Vocabulary#

✅ 4. Grammar & Phrase Patterns#

1) Pointing to Items#

  • これをください。(I’ll take this, please.)

  • それはちょっとたかいです。(That’s a little expensive.)

2) Asking for Availability#

  • メニューはありますか？(Do you have a menu?)

  • おちゃはありますか？(Do you have tea?)

3) Asking for Price#

  • これはいくらですか？(How much is this?)

  • コーヒーはいくらですか？(How much is the coffee?)

✅ 5. Mini-Dialogues (Practice Speaking)#

Dialogue 1 – Ordering Food
A: メニューをください。
B: はい、どうぞ。
A: このカレーをください。

Dialogue 2 – Asking Price
A: これはいくらですか？
B: 500えんです。

Dialogue 3 – Polite Decline
A: このケーキはちょっとたかいですね。
B: はい、すこし。
A: じゃあ、けっこうです。

✅ 6. Exercises#

Exercise 1 – Translate into Japanese#

  1. I’ll take this.

  2. Do you have beer?

  3. How much is that?

  4. That’s a little expensive.

  5. Then, no thank you.

Exercise 2 – Match the Phrases#

Match the Japanese to English:

  • いくらですか？ → ?

  • これをください → ?

  • けっこうです → ?

  • ちょっとたかいです → ?

  • メニュー → ?

Exercise 3 – Make Your Own Dialogues#

  1. Create your own short conversation asking for:

    • A drink

    • The price of an item

    • Something you decide not to buy

Exercise 4 – Speaking Practice#

  1. Say all food & drink nouns aloud 3× each.

  2. Practice asking prices with different nouns.

  3. Shadow Dialogues 1–3 at least 3 times.

✅ 7. Listening & Shadowing Practice#

Use the interactive audio buttons or MP3 (if generated) to:

  • Listen → Pause → Repeat

  • Shadow each phrase, especially shopping expressions

  • Practice reacting politely (e.g., けっこうです)

✅ 8. Review & Self-Check#

At the end of today, you should be able to:

  • Identify and say 10+ food/drink nouns

  • Ask for items, prices, and menus politely

  • Respond to prices and decline politely when needed
