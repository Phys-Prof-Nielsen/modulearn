Day 5: Family, Directions, and Time Phrases#

✅ 1. Goal for Today#

  • Learn how to talk about family members politely

  • Understand and give basic directions

  • Ask and tell the time in daily conversation

✅ 2. Family Vocabulary#

mother

おとうさん

father

おねえさん

older sister

おにいさん

older brother

✅ 5. Grammar & Usage Patterns#

Asking Time#

  • いま、なんじですか？ → What time is it now?

  • ごぜん or ごご can be added before the time:

    • ごぜんいちじ → 1 A.M.

    • ごごさんじ → 3 P.M.

Giving Directions#

  • 〜はどこですか？ → Where is ~?

    • えきはどこですか？ → Where is the station?

  • Combine location words:

    • まっすぐいって、みぎです → Go straight, then right.

✅ 6. Mini-Dialogues#

Dialogue 1 – Asking for Directions
A: えきはどこですか？
B: まっすぐいって、みぎです。

Dialogue 2 – Asking the Time
A: いま、なんじですか？
B: はちじはんです。

Dialogue 3 – Talking About Family
A: おにいさんはなんさいですか？
B: じゅうごさいです。

✅ 7. Exercises#

Exercise 1 – Translate into Japanese#

  1. Where is the station?

  2. It’s 7:00 A.M.

  3. Your older sister is 20 years old.

  4. Go straight, then left.

  5. What time is it now?

Exercise 3 – Write Your Own#

  1. Ask where something is (restaurant, school, etc.)

  2. Say the time and whether it’s A.M. or P.M.

  3. Describe a family member and their age

Exercise 4 – Speaking Practice#

  1. Say each family member out loud 3×

  2. Practice pointing and saying:

    • ここ / そこ / あそこ

    • みぎ / ひだり / まっすぐ

  3. Ask and answer time questions (with or without a watch)

✅ 8. Listening & Shadowing Practice#

Use the interactive audio for Day 5 or MP3 file:

  • Repeat each term aloud after listening

  • Practice all 3 mini-dialogues out loud

  • Shadow twice, then try from memory

✅ 9. Review & Self-Check#

By the end of today, you should be able to:

  • Refer to other people’s family members politely

  • Ask for and give directions with confidence

  • Ask and tell the time in Japanese clearly

from gtts import gTTS
from IPython.display import display, Audio
import io

# ✅ Day 5: Family, Directions, and Time Phrases
day5_sentences = [
    # --- Family Vocabulary ---
    "おかあさん。",  # mother
    "おとうさん。",  # father
    "おねえさん。",  # older sister
    "おにいさん。",  # older brother
    "むすめさん。",  # daughter
    "むすこさん。",  # son

    # --- Direction Vocabulary ---
    "みぎ。",       # right
    "ひだり。",     # left
    "まっすぐ。",   # straight
    "ここ。",       # here
    "そこ。",       # there
    "あそこ。",     # over there

    # --- Time Vocabulary & Phrases ---
    "いま。",           # now
    "なんじですか？",   # What time is it?
    "いちじです。",     # It is 1 o'clock
    "はちじはんです。", # It's 8:30
    "ごぜんですか？",   # Is it A.M.?
    "ごごです。",       # It's P.M.

    # --- Mini Dialogue 1 – Asking for Directions ---
    "えきはどこですか？",
    "まっすぐいって、みぎです。",

    # --- Mini Dialogue 2 – Asking Time ---
    "いま、なんじですか？",
    "はちじはんです。",

    # --- Mini Dialogue 3 – Talking about Family ---
    "おにいさんはなんさいですか？",
    "じゅうごさいです。"
]

# ✅ Generate audio and embed play buttons
for i, sentence in enumerate(day5_sentences):
    print(f"Sentence {i+1}: {sentence}")
    tts = gTTS(text=sentence, lang='ja')
    mp3_fp = io.BytesIO()
    tts.write_to_fp(mp3_fp)
    mp3_fp.seek(0)
    display(Audio(mp3_fp.read(), autoplay=False))
