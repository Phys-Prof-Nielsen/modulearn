Day 7: Feelings, Reactions, and Casual Chatting#

✅ 1. Goal for Today#

  • Express feelings and reactions naturally

  • Learn basic emotional and sensory adjectives

  • Practice casual interjections and responses

  • Build short, expressive conversations in both polite and friendly tones

✅ 2. Emotion & Sensation Vocabulary#

✅ 3. Common Reactions & Interjections#

Japanese

English

ほんとう？

Really?

そうですか。

I see.

そうですね。

Right… / Let me think…

いいですね！

That’s nice!

すごい！

Wow! / Amazing!

ええ〜！

Whaat?! (surprised or doubtful)

たしかに。

True / I agree

ちょっと…

Hmm… / Not really… (soft refusal)

だいじょうぶです。

I’m okay. / It’s fine.

✅ 4. Grammar Notes#

Polite vs Casual Adjective Endings#

  • Polite:
    　たのしいです。 → It’s fun.
    　さびしいです。 → I’m lonely.

  • Casual (omit です):
    　たのしい〜！ → So fun!
    　うれしい〜！ → I’m happy!

✅ 5. Mini Conversations#

Conversation 1 – Good News
A: にほんにいきます！
B: ほんとう？すごい！たのしんでね！

Conversation 2 – Tired
A: ねむいです…
B: だいじょうぶ？
A: うん、だいじょうぶ。

Conversation 3 – Compliment
A: このケーキ、おいしいですね！
B: そうですね。わたしもすきです。

✅ 6. Exercises#

Exercise 1 – Translate Your Reactions#

Translate into Japanese:

  1. That’s amazing!

  2. I’m tired.

  3. I’m a little sad.

  4. This food is delicious!

  5. I’m okay.

Exercise 2 – Complete the Dialogue#

A: ______？ (Really?)
B: うん、ほんとう。
A: ______！(Wow!)

Exercise 3 – Create Your Own#

Make a 3-line conversation using:

  • 1 emotion

  • 1 reaction

  • 1 casual follow-up

✅ 7. Speaking & Listening Practice#

  • Say each reaction 3× with different tones (happy, surprised, sleepy)

  • Practice each mini conversation aloud

  • Roleplay a tired or happy situation with your own ending

✅ 8. Self-Check#

Can you…

  • React naturally to good or bad news?

  • Use emotional adjectives in polite and casual form?

  • Understand short casual conversations?

✅ 9. Bonus Challenge#

Say the following in Japanese:

  • I’m really happy!

  • This food is amazing.

  • That’s a little scary…

  • I’m okay, thanks.

  • That’s true.

✅ 10. Listening Practice Tip#

Try watching a slice-of-life anime scene or J-Drama with subtitles and listen for:

  • たのしい！

  • すごい！

  • だいじょうぶ？

  • ほんとう？
    Write them down and mimic their tone!

from gtts import gTTS
from IPython.display import display, Audio
import io

# ✅ Day 7 Sentences – Feelings, Reactions, and Mini Conversations
day7_sentences = [
    # --- Emotions ---
    "うれしい",  # happy
    "たのしい",  # fun
    "さびしい",  # lonely
    "かなしい",  # sad

    "まずい",    # bad-tasting
    "こわい",    # scary
    "すごい",    # amazing
    "たいへん",  # hard/difficult
    "ねむい",    # sleepy

    # --- Common Reactions ---
    "ほんとう？",
    "そうですか。",
    "そうですね。",
    "いいですね！",
    "すごい！",
    "ええ〜！",

    "ちょっと...",
    "だいじょうぶです。",

    # --- Mini Conversation 2 ---

    # --- Mini Conversation 3 ---

    "そうですね。わたしもすきです。"
]

# ✅ Generate audio for each and display play button
for i, sentence in enumerate(day7_sentences):
    print(f"Sentence {i+1}: {sentence}")
    tts = gTTS(text=sentence, lang='ja')
    mp3_fp = io.BytesIO()
    tts.write_to_fp(mp3_fp)
    mp3_fp.seek(0)
    display(Audio(mp3_fp.read(), autoplay=False))
