# A Competency Approach to Classical Mechanics: A Compendium to the C Exam

This Level C e-book marks the transition from foundational tools to the broader 
landscape of Classical Mechanics, where the methods become both more powerful and 
more abstract. Building on the groundwork laid in Level D, we now turn to systems 
and frameworks that extend beyond the reach of Newton’s second law in its familiar 
form. Here, we explore variable mass systems where impulse-momentum becomes the fundament instead of
$\vec{f} = m\vec{a}$, rotating frames where fictitious forces emerge as natural consequences of 
non-inertial motion, and the method of Lagrange multipliers that systematically 
incorporates constraints.

At this level, we also begin our journey into the Hamiltonian formulation of 
mechanics, opening the door to the language of modern field theory of physics. 
Two-body and central force dynamics deepen our understanding of interactions 
ranging from planetary orbits to molecular binding, while the inclusion of 
numerical methods reflects the essential role of computation in contemporary 
problem solving.

The material in this e-book highlights the shift from applying formulas to 
mastering frameworks: each topic reveals not only new techniques but also new 
perspectives on the unity of mechanics. By the end of Level C, you will be 
equipped with a versatile set of analytical and computational tools, prepared 
to confront the higher abstractions of Levels B and A, and ready to see 
mechanics as both a finished classical theory and a bridge to the frontiers 
of physics.
