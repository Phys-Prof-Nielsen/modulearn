Problem 4: Matching Motion Graphs to Descriptions#

Consider the following motion scenarios and determine the expected position-time and velocity-time graphs:

  1. A car moves at a constant speed of 10 m/s along a straight road.

  2. A ball is dropped from a height and accelerates due to gravity.

  3. A cyclist starts from rest and speeds up at a constant rate.

For each scenario:

  • Identify whether the position-time and velocity-time graphs are linear or curved.

  • Sketch the expected graphs and label key points.
