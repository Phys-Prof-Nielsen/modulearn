# Notes for University Physics II Lab

This laboratory course is designed to accompany University Physics II, providing hands-on 
experimental experience that reinforces fundamental concepts in electricity, magnetism, optics, 
and modern physics. Students engage in a sequence of structured experiments emphasizing accurate 
data collection, uncertainty analysis, and the development of scientific reasoning skills.

The course begins with an introduction to measurement techniques, experimental uncertainty, 
and computational data analysis tools. Subsequent experiments explore electric fields and 
potentials, resistive and capacitive circuits, magnetic fields and forces, and electromagnetic 
applications. Later laboratories investigate geometrical and physical optics, including 
polarization and interference, as well as selected topics from modern physics.

Throughout the course, strong emphasis is placed on experimental design, proper use of 
laboratory instrumentation, and the interpretation of results using graphical and 
computational methods. Students are expected to maintain clear and well-organized electronic 
lab notebooks, collaborate effectively, and communicate their findings through professional-quality 
written reports, fostering both technical proficiency and a deeper conceptual understanding of physical principles.
