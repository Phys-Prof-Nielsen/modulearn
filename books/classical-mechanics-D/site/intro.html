A Competency Approach to Classical Mechanics: A Compendium to the D Exam#

This Level D e-book marks the foundation of our journey into Classical Mechanics, where we revisit familiar ideas from introductory physics and reshape them into a deeper, more systematic framework. At this stage, our focus is on building the essential tools and language of the subject: the concepts of measurement and modeling, the role of constraints and generalized coordinates, and the dynamics of particles in different coordinate systems. By working through these topics, students will begin to see physics not as a collection of formulas, but as a coherent structure rooted in first principles. The material in this e-book is deliberately chosen to balance accessibility with rigor, preparing you for the progressively more abstract and powerful methods introduced in Levels C, B, and A. Here, you will lay the groundwork for understanding how classical mechanics connects not only to advanced physics but also to the modern theories that shape our view of the universe.

NOTE: this is not a complete set of notes and is not a substitution for your textbook.
