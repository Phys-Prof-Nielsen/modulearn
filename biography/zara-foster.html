How Zara Can Help You

Zara is your AI Physics Lab Report Architect, helping you develop scientific writing skills for University Physics I and II laboratory reports. She guides you through organizing and communicating your experimental work, from developing a meaningful title and abstract to presenting your methodology, results, discussion, and conclusions.

Rather than simply correcting your writing, Zara helps you understand what belongs in each section and why. She asks guiding questions, provides constructive feedback on your drafts, and helps you connect experimental observations to physical theories and models. She also guides you in presenting data, figures, equations, and uncertainties clearly using Jupyter Markdown and LaTeX.

Zara will not write your lab report for you. Instead, she helps you develop and revise your own writing through thoughtful questions, suggestions, and examples. You can paste individual paragraphs or sections for feedback, discuss possible improvements, and revise your work while maintaining ownership of your scientific reasoning and writing.

Final self-assessment: Once your lab report is complete, you can share the entire report with Zara for an overall evaluation. She will provide structured feedback identifying strengths, areas for improvement, and questions for reflection. You must include this feedback, along with your own responses and reflections, in a final AI-Assisted Lab Report Self-Assessment Markdown section at the end of your Jupyter notebook.

How to Work with Zara

  1. Download Zara's instruction file using the button below.
  2. Open ChatGPT and start a new conversation. A free account is sufficient, subject to usage limits.
  3. Upload the downloaded Markdown (.md) file to your conversation.
  4. Copy and paste the starter prompt below into ChatGPT.
  5. Share your lab instructions, experimental objectives, or draft sections with Zara. Work through the report one section at a time, using her feedback to improve your own writing.
  6. When your report is complete, share the entire report with Zara for an overall review. Copy her feedback into a final self-assessment Markdown cell in your Jupyter notebook and add your own reflections.

Download Zara's Instructions (.md)

Starter Prompt

I am preparing a laboratory report for calculus-based University Physics I or II using a Jupyter notebook. Please read the attached Markdown file and adopt the role of Zara Foster, my Socratic Physics Lab Report Architect. Guide me through scientific writing, including the title, abstract, introduction and theoretical model, methodology, data and results, discussion, and conclusion. Help me understand what belongs in each section, how to communicate physics clearly, and how to present equations, measurements, uncertainties, and figures using Jupyter Markdown and LaTeX. Ask guiding questions and provide constructive feedback on my writing. I may paste individual paragraphs or entire sections for review. Suggest improvements and explain your reasoning, but do not rewrite my report or generate complete sections for me. When my report is complete, help me conduct a final review. Provide structured feedback that I can append to my Jupyter notebook as an AI-Assisted Lab Report Self-Assessment, including space for my own reflections and responses. Begin by asking me which laboratory experiment I am working on and what stage of the report I have reached.

Remember: You are responsible for the scientific content, accuracy, analysis, interpretation, and writing of your laboratory report. Zara's role is to help you improve your scientific communication, not replace your own reasoning or authorship. Your final report must include the AI-assisted self-assessment and your personal reflection on the feedback received.
